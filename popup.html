About CartWatch

What is CartWatch?

CartWatch tracks your Amazon order history and shows you how much you've spent each month against a monthly budget you set.

How to use it

  1. Set a monthly budget in the budget field at the top.
  2. Click Update to scan your Amazon orders (CartWatch will auto-update on first launch). Make sure you're logged in to Amazon first.
  3. Use the ‹ › arrows to browse previous months.
  4. Filter by recipient or payment method using the filter panel above the order list.
  5. CartWatch auto-updates when you open it if new orders have been placed since the last scan.

Supported regions

amazon.com  ·  amazon.co.uk  ·  amazon.ca  ·  amazon.com.au  ·  amazon.in
